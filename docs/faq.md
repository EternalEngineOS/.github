# FAQ

Mirrored from the FAQ on [eternalengineos.io](https://eternalengineos.io) (home page and
pricing page). For anything not covered here, see [`SUPPORT.md`](../SUPPORT.md).

**What is EternalEngine?**
An operating system for service businesses that combines CRM, project management,
invoicing, scheduling, AI assistance, and analytics into a single platform — for solo
contractors through 200-person teams.

**Who is EternalEngine for?**
Small businesses, growing teams, and larger organizations across construction, field
service, professional services, and general business operations.

**How does pricing work?**
Six plans, flat monthly pricing, extra seats as an optional add-on, and a 30-day free trial
on every paid plan. Annual billing saves 20%. See [`docs/pricing.md`](pricing.md).

**Is there a free plan?**
Yes. Free is one person, the full CRM, 5 invoices and 5 estimates a month, and no card
required, with no time limit.

**How does the 30-day free trial work?**
Create an account on any paid plan and your first 30 days are free. A card is required at
signup so your workspace continues without interruption, but nothing is charged until the
trial ends — you're emailed three days before that date, and you can cancel any time before
then from Settings → Billing to pay $0.

**What does the AI assistant do?**
It provides predictive lead scoring, revenue forecasting, automated task routing, natural
language data queries, and recommendations based on your own business data.

**Can I import my existing data?**
Yes — CSV import for contacts, leads, deals, and other entities. Pro and Business add
field mapping and duplicate detection.

**Is my data secure?**
Encrypted in transit and at rest, with row-level security, role-based access controls, and
full audit trails enforced at the database layer. See
[`docs/security-and-compliance.md`](security-and-compliance.md).

**Do you offer white-labeling?**
Yes, on the Business plan — custom domains, presented as your own branded platform.

**What about apps marked "Coming Soon" in the store?**
They're visible so you can read what they do; install opens app-by-app as each one reaches
launch readiness, at no extra charge to your paid plan.

**Where do I ask something not on this list?**
[Contact us](https://eternalengineos.io/contact) or see [`SUPPORT.md`](../SUPPORT.md).
