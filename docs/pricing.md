## What's included at every tier

- **Free** — the full CRM (ClientForge), PayGate payments at the Free processing tier, and
  ThreadRealm community forums. Best for trying it before you commit a dollar.
- **Basic** — everything in Free, plus workflow rules and a public booking page (BookSlot).
  Best for a one-person shop. Start here: [eternalengineos.io/basic](https://eternalengineos.io/basic).
- **Team** — everything in Basic, plus more booking pages with calendar sync, WikiShelf,
  NeuralGrid (AI command center), and scheduled workflow actions. Best for a crew of 2–3.
- **Pro** — everything in Team, plus BidForge (estimates), ProjectPath (projects and time
  tracking), AssetVault (file storage), TitanLedger (invoicing), and CrewGrid (field service
  dispatch). Best for growing teams up to 5.
- **Business** — everything in Pro, plus FlowRelay (workflow automation), forecasting, AI
  insights and lead scoring, advanced reporting, white-label branding with custom domains,
  and SSO/SAML. Best for established operations, 6–15 people.
- **Enterprise** — custom seats, volume and usage caps, negotiated annual contracts,
  SSO/SAML with dedicated support, white-glove onboarding, and a custom SLA.
  [Contact us](https://eternalengineos.io/contact).

## Add-ons

Add-ons exist only for usage overages, not for unlocking apps: extra email volume, extra
AI credits, and extra seats. EternalEngine is one subscription, not a marketplace of
separately billed apps.
