# App Catalog

EternalEngine ships 17 apps at launch under one login and one subscription — plus a growing
catalog of add-on apps that install at no extra charge as they reach launch readiness. Every
app's full feature page is linked below.

## Universal — included on every plan

| App | Category | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [ScrollForge](https://eternalengineos.io/features/scrollforge/) | In-App Store | Browse, install, and manage every app from one catalog. |
| [ClientForge](https://eternalengineos.io/features/clientforge/) | CRM | Contacts, deals, and pipelines — every relationship in one place. |
| [ThreadRealm](https://eternalengineos.io/features/threadrealm/) | Community Forums | Cross-customer boards where your community compounds. |
| [PostFrame](https://eternalengineos.io/features/postframe/) | Email Delivery | Transactional and marketing email with tracking, no third party. |
| [PayGate](https://eternalengineos.io/features/paygate/) | Payments | Native checkout, subscriptions, and payouts at one all-in rate — no separate processor fee. |
| [NeuralGrid](https://eternalengineos.io/features/neuralgrid/) | AI Command Center | One AI layer routing across providers by cost — the brains behind every app's assistant. |
