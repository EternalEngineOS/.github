# Roadmap

We don't publish a public backlog or ETAs. What follows is only what our own site already
states publicly as coming — nothing more.

## Publicly stated as in progress or planned

| Item | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| GDPR compliance program | In progress | [Security page](https://eternalengineos.io/security) |
| SOC 2 Type II certification | Roadmap — not yet started/certified | [Security page](https://eternalengineos.io/security) |
| ISO 27001 certification | Roadmap — not yet started/certified | [Security page](https://eternalengineos.io/security) |
| Automated, public status page with uptime history | Coming with the monitoring rollout | [Status page](https://eternalengineos.io/status) |
| Published incident post-mortems | Will appear on the status page when an incident occurs | [Status page](https://eternalengineos.io/status) |

We are explicit about this on our own site: "we will never display a certification we
haven't earned — when these flip to 'certified,' it will be real."

## How we share the roadmap

- **Shipped work** is in the [changelog](https://eternalengineos.io/changelog) and
  [`CHANGELOG.md`](CHANGELOG.md), dated and versioned.
- **What's stable vs. still moving** is on the
  [beta program page](https://eternalengineos.io/beta).
- **Reliability commitments** (the 99.9% uptime target, what's monitored today) are on the
  [status page](https://eternalengineos.io/status).

We don't maintain a public issue tracker or project board for the product roadmap — the
product repository is private. If you want to weigh in on what we build next, use
[ThreadRealm](https://eternalengineos.io/features/threadrealm/) in-app, or
[contact us](https://eternalengineos.io/contact).
